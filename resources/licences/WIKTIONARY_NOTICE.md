# Wiktionary and Kaikki attribution

Frequency-data candidates may be extracted from Wiktionary through the
machine-readable datasets published by Kaikki.org and generated with
Wiktextract.

- Kaikki dictionary index: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/index.html
- Raw data downloads: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/rawdata.html
- Wiktextract: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract
- Wiktionary copyright: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights

The extracted dictionary content remains available under the same licences as
Wiktionary: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 and the GNU Free
Documentation License. Every derived production record must retain a source
URL and the exact Wiktionary dump or Kaikki extraction revision used.

The project also records the Wiktextract citation requested by Kaikki.org:
Tatu Ylonen, “Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data,”
Proceedings of LREC 2022, pages 1317–1325.

Human review is mandatory before a candidate enters the production corpus.
Dictionary extraction and frequency scoring do not by themselves establish
translation accuracy, regional suitability, or pedagogical quality.
