# Thai frequency-data notice

The Thai production rankings are compiled from redistributable sources:

- Phupha Thai Word Frequency Dataset, Common Crawl July 2025 counts, dataset
  dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Source and citation:
  https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/Phupha-Word-freq and
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18490474
- FrequencyWords OpenSubtitles 2018 Thai frequency data, content distributed
  under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
- Thai Wiktionary lexical data extracted by Kaikki/Wiktextract under Wiktionary's
  CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL terms: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Thai/

The Lenguia and 1000MostCommonWords.com pages supplied for comparison do not state
compatible redistribution terms. The supplied Scribd document is marked “All
Rights Reserved.” Their word lists are not copied into the application.

Thai script records are filtered dictionary entries. The separate Latin-script
option uses only the tone-marked Paiboon romanization included in the Wiktionary
data; records without an explicit Paiboon form are skipped.
