# Audio delivery options — deferred proposal

Recorded: 2026-10-13
Status: proposal retained for evaluation. The user explicitly deferred implementation.

## Downloadable language packs

Keep a small core app. On language selection, offer a one-time download of that
language's default-deck text and compressed audio. Show size and progress.
After download, cards, lists and audio study play local files offline. Permit
removing and redownloading packs. Male/female audio may be separate choices.
Generated decks can receive server-generated audio alongside their text; after
both downloads finish they remain playable offline. Internet is needed for
generation and initial downloads, not for replay.

This is analogous to Babbel's documented offline-lesson downloads, not a claim
about Babbel's private speech-generation implementation:
https://support.babbel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19176178120722-Offline-mode

Expected benefits: consistent chosen voices across devices, inspectable default
pronunciation, no dependency on phone TTS voice configuration, bounded core app
size. Costs include audio generation, storage, transfer and additional disk
space. Preliminary estimates of 12–24 MB per language per voice assume 1,000
word/sentence pairs, 4–8 seconds per pair and 24 kbps audio. These are assumptions,
not measurements. Validate intelligibility, codec support and actual duration.

## Current TTS evidence

Android 0.6.3 reaches Google's TTS engine and reports its native error. The user
confirmed English plays; the Spanish card-language test reports error -4.
Android defines -4 as ERROR_SERVICE. This identifies a speech-service failure;
it does not establish missing voice data as the cause. Spanish voice assets,
the selected locale/voice, or a network-dependent voice remain hypotheses.

The 0.6.3 DeviceSpeech selection ranks offline voices but only applies an
explicit voice when its gender is identifiable. For opaque Android voice names,
it keeps the engine default selected by setLanguage. That default may require a
network connection. This is a confirmed selection weakness, not a confirmed
explanation of the user's specific service error. Advertised language support
does not prove that usable offline voice data is installed.

Generated and default decks share the phone speech path unless transferred
audio clips exist. Previously working generated decks do not prove that Spanish
voice data is currently missing.

## Quality and offline behavior

Downloaded recordings may themselves be generated by TTS. Quality depends on
the chosen voice/model, pronunciation, text normalization and audio compression;
recording versus live synthesis alone does not determine quality. Pre-generated
default audio can be reviewed before distribution and stays consistent across
phones. Phone TTS offers flexible speech for arbitrary text with quality that
depends on the installed engine/voice.

Local installed TTS voices can synthesize offline. Network voices require a
connection. Cached audio files play offline after download. Bundling a small
offline multilingual engine is another possible approach; check every supported
language, pronunciation and distribution requirements before selection.

## Interim TTS correction — 0.6.4

Android explicitly selects an installed offline voice, including opaque voice
names without gender metadata. Network-only and notInstalled voices are excluded.
Up to three matching voices are attempted after selection or synthesis failures.
Regional fallbacks and Norwegian, Mandarin and Indonesian engine aliases are
recognized; a missing language never silently falls back to English. Stopping
playback invalidates pending retries. Android setVoice selects the locale directly
without temporarily selecting the engine's potentially network-dependent default.

Regression tests cover all 24 configured language variants, regional/alias
matching, engine selection and synthesis failures, missing offline data and
cancellation. These are automated selection tests, not physical-phone
pronunciation checks. Installed offline voices remain a phone prerequisite; an
app patch cannot supply missing engine assets. Audio-pack implementation remains
deferred. Re-test Spanish with Google speech services and airplane mode on the
affected phone before treating its native service error as resolved.
